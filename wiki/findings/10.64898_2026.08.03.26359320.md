# The Neuropsychiatry of 22q11.2 Deletion Syndrome: An Electronic Health Records Study.

## Key Findings

A retrospective study in the TriNetX Global Collaborative Network identified 10,831 individuals with clinical codes consistent with 22q11.2 deletion syndrome and compared them with propensity score-matched controls. Neurodevelopmental diagnoses were over-represented: intellectual disability OR 33.2 (95% CI 24.4-45.2), autism spectrum disorder OR 5.4 (4.6-6.2), developmental language disorder OR 6.1 (5.6-6.7).

## Relevance to Atlas

A large CNV cohort assembled from routine records rather than clinical ascertainment, which is the main claim to novelty since ascertained 22q11.2 cohorts are enriched for the most affected. The odds ratios are relative to matched healthcare controls, so they are prevalence contrasts within a healthcare-using population, not population penetrance estimates. Diagnosis is by billing code, which mismeasures both the deletion and the psychiatric outcomes.

## Genes

None from the 49-gene autism set.

## Methods

Retrospective observational study; TriNetX Global Collaborative Network; propensity score matching; diagnostic code prevalence and odds.
