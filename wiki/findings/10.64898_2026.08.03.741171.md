# Granule microenvironment regulates the dual functions of FMR1

## Key Findings

FMR1 was identified as part of the oskar mRNA-protein complex in the Drosophila oocyte, acting as a translational enhancer rather than the repressor role it is usually assigned, with the granule microenvironment determining which function dominates.

## Relevance to Atlas

Marginal for this atlas. Logged because it complicates the standard directional assumption about FMR1 function, and directional assumptions about gene function are a recurring source of error in interpreting network results here. Drosophila oocyte, not neural.
