# Developmental CA2 perineuronal net reduction restores social memory in Shank3 mutant mice.

## Key Findings
Shank3B KO mice have excessive CA2 perineuronal nets during development, sequestering semaphorin-3A and disrupting afferent input; reducing PNNs restores afferents and social recognition into adulthood.

## Relevance to Atlas
Cell-type-resolved (CA2) developmental mechanism for an atlas core gene (SHANK3) with a reversible early-intervention angle. Mechanistic, not regulatory-variant.

## Genes
SHANK3

## Methods
Shank3B knockout mice; PNN quantification, semaphorin-3A and afferent tracing, PNN reduction, social recognition behavior.
