# Autism-specific spliceosomal transcriptomic signatures in prefrontal cortex contrasted with bipolar disorder.

## Key Findings

Comparative RNA-seq of postmortem prefrontal cortex found 45 differentially expressed genes in ASD, mostly downregulated small nuclear and small nucleolar RNAs, with enrichment for RNA processing and spliceosome assembly. Bipolar disorder samples showed a different profile: 12 candidate genes, mostly upregulated protein-coding, enriched for metal ion response, vascular regulation and inflammation. No differentially expressed genes and no enriched GO biological process terms were shared between the two conditions.

## Relevance to Atlas

The ASD signal is carried by noncoding RNA genes rather than protein-coding genes, which is the class of variation this atlas tracks. Sample sizes and the source cohorts are not stated in the abstract, and a bulk-tissue postmortem contrast has no cell-type resolution, so this is descriptive rather than a nulled result. Pairs with the snRNA variant-classification preprint logged the same day (10.64898/2026.08.03.26359558).

## Genes

None from the 49-gene autism set.

## Methods

RNA-seq of postmortem prefrontal cortex; DESeq2 differential expression with batch covariate in the BD model; GO over-representation, KEGG GSEA, gene-concept network visualization.
