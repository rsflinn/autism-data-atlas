# Impaired Neuregulin 1 Processing is Associated with Synaptic and Behavioral Abnormalities in a Prenatal Valproic Acid Model of Autism.

## Key Findings
In a prenatal valproic acid rat model, reduced cleaved NRG1 (without transcript change) and decreased sheddases ADAM10/ADAM17/BACE1 attenuated ErbB4-AKT/ERK signalling, reduced synaptic scaffolding, and impaired spine maturation in hippocampus. Recombinant NRG1 restored ErbB4 signalling and improved ASD-relevant behaviours, with sex-specific social deficits.

## Relevance to Atlas
Prenatal valproic acid model links impaired NRG1 processing to E/I imbalance and synaptic/behavioural deficits; NRG1 administration rescues phenotypes. No 49-set gene; relevant to the E/I-balance mechanism the atlas tracks.
