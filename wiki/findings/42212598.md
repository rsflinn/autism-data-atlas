# Childhood trauma as a mediator between autistic traits and depression: Evidence from the ALSPAC birth cohort.

## Key Findings
Using ALSPAC (up to 9,659 individuals followed to age 28) with autism diagnosis, autism polygenic score, and trait measures, all autism variables showed increased depression trajectories ages 10-28. Social communication difficulties most strongly predicted adult depression; trauma and autistic traits combined additively. Mediation showed direct and trauma-mediated pathways.

## Relevance to Atlas
Population cohort (ALSPAC) using autism polygenic score and trait measures shows additive trauma-by-traits effects on adult depression. Relevant to the atlas's common-variant/polygenic architecture model; no specific 49-set gene.
