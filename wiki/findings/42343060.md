# Epigenetic Dysregulation of the Wnt/beta-Catenin Pathway via FTO-m6A in the mPFC Contributes to Autism-Like Behaviors.

## Key Findings
In BTBR autism-model mice the RNA demethylase FTO is upregulated in medial prefrontal cortex; knocking it down rescues core ASD-like behaviors. m6A methylome mapping points to Ctnnb1 (Wnt/beta-catenin) as a target, linking RNA-level epigenetic regulation to autism behavior.
