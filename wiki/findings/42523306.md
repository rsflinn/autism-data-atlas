# The Disease Gene THAP12 is a Transcriptional Regulator of Mitochondrial ETC Complex I.

## Key Findings
Genome-wide CRISPR screens identify THAP12 as a nuclear DNA-binding activator of Complex I assembly genes. Patient fibroblasts with THAP12 mutations show the same transcriptional defect and profound CI deficiency, reframing THAP12-associated NDD as a secondary mitochondrial disease.

## Relevance to Atlas
Low autism specificity. THAP12 is not in the 49-gene set and there is no autism-specific evidence here. Cleared the numeric threshold on dataset quality and novelty, not on autism relevance. Logged in case mitochondrial Complex I becomes relevant as a downstream convergence point.
