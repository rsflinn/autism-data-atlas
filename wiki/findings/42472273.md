# Shared genetics between ADHD and reading/language abilities: Genome-wide correlations, stratified enrichment, cross-trait association, and mendelian randomization.

## Key Findings
Integrated PGC ADHD GWAS (38,691 cases; 186,843 controls) with GenLang language/reading GWAS. Genome-wide genetic correlations were negative and significant between ADHD and word reading, nonword reading, spelling and phoneme awareness. Stratified LDSC showed heritability enrichment concentrated in conserved/constrained annotations (GERP, phastCons); cross-trait loci mapped near neurodevelopmental genes including DCC, MEF2C and ST3GAL3. Authors caution effect sizes are modest.

## Relevance to Atlas
Directly relevant to the common-variant noncoding architecture line: heritability enrichment sits in conserved/constrained regulatory annotations, and MEF2C (in the 49-gene set) appears among cross-trait loci. ADHD not autism, so relevance is transdiagnostic; public GWAS summary stats are usable.
