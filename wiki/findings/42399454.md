# GATOR1 signaling defects promote astrocytic metabolic rewiring and excitatory neurotransmitter cycling.

## Key Findings
Loss of GATOR1 (an mTORC1 brake mutated in epilepsy, infantile spasms and ASD) in astrocytes disrupts mitochondrial metabolism and increases glutamate uptake and glutamine synthesis, tying astrocytic mTOR signaling to excitatory network homeostasis and seizures. Relevant to the somatic mTOR-pathway line of inquiry.
