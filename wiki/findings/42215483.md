# Glycine-modulating Slc6a20a-ASO restores NMDA receptor function in SHANK2 and SHANK3-mutant mice and cortical organoids.

## Key Findings
Antisense oligonucleotide inhibition of the glycine transporter Slc6a20a normalizes ASD-related phenotypes in Shank2- and Shank3-mutant mice, rescuing NMDAR hypofunction and synaptic phospho-proteomic profiles in prefrontal cortex. Human SLC6A20-targeting ASO rescues NMDAR function in SHANK2/SHANK3-mutant cortical organoids.

## Relevance to Atlas
ASO knockdown of glycine transporter Slc6a20a/SLC6A20 restores NMDAR function and rescues ASD phenotypes in Shank2/Shank3 mice and human cortical organoids. SHANK3 is in the 49-gene set; relevance is therapeutic/mechanistic, not variant-level.
