# Molecular determinants of functional voltage-gated sodium (Nav) channel diversity in mammalian central neurons.

## Key Findings
Review of how Nav1.1/1.2/1.6 alpha subunits combine with Navbeta, iFGF and ankyrin auxiliary proteins to produce cell-type-specific sodium currents in central neurons. Variants in SCN1A, SCN2A and SCN8A plus auxiliary-protein genes are linked to epilepsy, ataxia and neurodevelopmental disease. No primary data, no statistical test.

## Relevance to Atlas
Bears on the ion-channel finding that replicates in this project: ion channel disruption as a final common pathway across NDD gene knockouts. This review supplies the cell-type and subcellular resolution behind that phenomenon, describing which Nav alpha subunits and auxiliary proteins are expressed in cortical pyramidal neurons, cortical interneurons, cerebellar Purkinje and granule neurons. It also restates that SCN1A, SCN2A and SCN8A variants span epilepsy and neurodevelopmental diagnoses, consistent with the individual-gene overlap that survived testing here while class-level receptor separation did not.

Limits: this is a narrative review, not primary data. There is no null model, no base rate and no statistical test, so nothing here can raise or lower confidence in any atlas hypothesis. Treat as background and as a source of testable cell-type predictions, not as evidence.

## Genes
SCN1A, SCN2A, SCN8A

## Methods
Narrative review of published electrophysiology, molecular and genetic literature on Nav channel alpha and auxiliary subunits in mammalian central neurons.
