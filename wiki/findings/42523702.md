# The RNA-binding protein CELF4: from molecular regulatory networks to clinical translation in cross-system diseases.

## Key Findings
Review of CELF4 as a 3'UTR-binding translational repressor maintaining excitatory homeostasis in the CNS, with downregulation or promoter methylation implicated in autism, epilepsy and depression, and haploinsufficiency established in 18q12.2 microdeletion syndrome.

## Relevance to Atlas
Post-transcriptional and epigenetic control of synaptic and excitability targets is a regulatory layer this project's variant work does not currently cover, and CELF4 sits at the autism-epilepsy intersection that is the project's core question. It is a narrative review, so nothing here is independently nulled; treat as a pointer to primary literature rather than evidence.
