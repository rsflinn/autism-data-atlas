# Truncating ASXL1 variants rewire cellular metabolism via mitochondrial pyruvate carrier repression.

## Key Findings
Truncating ASXL1 variants cause aberrant PR-DUB complex spreading at an H3K4me3-marked intronic regulatory element in MPC1, producing a modest transcript reduction amplified into a large protein-level loss of MPC1/MPC2. Mitochondrial pyruvate restriction was placed causally upstream of Wnt signaling dysregulation.

## Relevance to Atlas
A worked example of a coding mutation acting through a specific noncoding regulatory element, with the effect amplified between transcript and protein. That amplification step is the kind of mechanism this project's threshold model requires but has not demonstrated. Bohring-Opitz is an NDD, not autism, so treat gene-set relevance as indirect.

## Genes
None of the 49-gene autism set are directly implicated.

## Methods
Patient-derived dermal fibroblasts, metabolic flux measurement, ChIP-seq of ASXL1/BAP1/BRD4/H3K4me3, MPC pharmacologic inhibition, Wnt pathway manipulation.
