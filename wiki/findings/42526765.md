# USP15 mutation associated with autism spectrum disorder alters progenitor fate and neuronal maturation in human brain organoids.

## Key Findings
Isogenic USP15-mutant human brain organoids show dosage-dependent disruption of progenitor fate and deep-layer neuron maturation. Regulon analysis found reduced activity of progenitor-associated regulons (SOX2, NR2F1, NR2F2) in heterozygotes. The mutant transcriptional signature overlapped Fezf2 and Foxp1 mutant signatures from a mouse brain perturbation atlas, and mutant-associated genes were enriched for known ASD risk genes.

## Relevance to Atlas
Direct evidence that a non-transcription-factor ASD gene converges on the same deep-layer projection-neuron program as FOXP1, one of the neurodevelopmental transcription factors this project has tracked. Relevant as an independent test of whether NDD gene perturbations converge transcriptionally. Note the convergence claim here is signature overlap, not a nulled specificity test.

FOXP1

## Methods
Isogenic iPSC-derived brain organoids (het and hom), single-cell RNA-seq, pseudotime, SCENIC-style regulon inference, comparison to mouse brain perturbation atlas.
