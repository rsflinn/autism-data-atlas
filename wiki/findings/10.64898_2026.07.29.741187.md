# Genetic and stochastic basis of phenotypic discordance in 16p11.2 mouse model deletion

## Key Findings

In an isogenic mouse model of the human 16p11.2 deletion, roughly 50% of del/+ mice showed increased seizure susceptibility to low-dose pentylenetetrazole and some showed spontaneous epileptiform activity. Whole-brain c-Fos imaging separated low and high responders: low responders showed minimal activation plus circuit activity in the thalamic reticular nucleus, pretectal region and pedunculopontine nucleus that the authors read as compensatory suppression.

## Relevance to Atlas

This is the closest thing today to the atlas guiding question, which is why the same genotype produces different phenotypes. The animals are isogenic and the environment is controlled, so the discordance cannot be attributed to background variants or environment, which narrows the explanation to stochastic developmental variation and compensatory circuit states. Caveat: the compensatory-circuit interpretation is correlational, an activation pattern in low responders, not a manipulation.
