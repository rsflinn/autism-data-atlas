# Linking chemical data from the Comparative Toxicogenomics Database with adverse outcome pathways from the AOP-Wiki: a mechanistic data-oriented approach to help inform environmental health.

## Key Findings
Intersecting CTD toxicogenomic data with the autism AOP identifies environmental chemicals (bisphenols, PFAS, pesticides, metals, air pollutants) and specific genes including CNTNAP2 that may modulate autism key events.

## Relevance to Atlas
Gene-environment layer; flags CNTNAP2 (atlas gene set) as a chemical-modulated node and provides a reusable database-integration method. No human regulatory-variant data.

## Genes
CNTNAP2

## Methods
Computational intersection of Comparative Toxicogenomics Database curated content with AOP-Wiki adverse outcome pathways.
