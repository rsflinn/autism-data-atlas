# Phosphoproteomic profiling reveals reversal of dysregulated kinase signaling by nitric oxide inhibition in the Shank3 mouse model of autism.

## Key Findings

Phosphoproteomics of Shank3 KO mouse cortex shows altered phosphorylation across synaptic and RNA-metabolism proteins, partly reversed by nNOS inhibition. Touches SHANK3 (49-gene set) and SFARI high-risk genes at the protein level. Mechanism/therapeutic-target work, not variant-level; mouse model only.
