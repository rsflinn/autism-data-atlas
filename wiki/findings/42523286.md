# A Role for Astrocyte Metabolism in Species-Specific Neuronal Development.

## Key Findings
Astrocyte metabolic differences between human and non-human primates causally influence neuronal maturation rate, with PHGDH inhibition partially reproducing the effect. Human astrocyte secretomes were enriched for synaptogenic and axon-growth proteins.
