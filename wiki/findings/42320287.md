# Bayesian colocalization of psychiatric disorder GWAS signals with molecular quantitative trait loci highlights histone involvement and neuroimmune dysregulation in pathogenesis.

## Key Findings
A multi-omics Bayesian colocalization framework tested GWAS signals from ADHD, ASD, bipolar disorder, major depression and schizophrenia against transcript, protein and metabolite QTL. Sixty-five schizophrenia, bipolar and depression signals colocalized with immune cell-type-specific eQTL targeting 70 genes, including 17 histone genes; downstream pQTL and metabQTL colocalization implicated immune pathways and GABAergic neurotransmission.

## Relevance to Atlas
Relevant as method and framing for moving noncoding risk loci to mechanism through QTL colocalization. The caveat for the atlas: the colocalized signals were concentrated in schizophrenia, bipolar and depression, not ASD, so the autism-specific yield here is limited and should not be overstated.

## Genes
None of the 49-gene autism set are named in the abstract.

## Methods
Bayesian colocalization (coloc) of GWAS with eQTL, pQTL and metabQTL across multi-omics layers; immune cell-type-specific eQTL.
