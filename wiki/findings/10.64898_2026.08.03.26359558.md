# Guidance for clinical variant classification in genes for spliceosomal small nuclear RNAs

## Key Findings

The group quantified an elevated background mutation rate in snRNA genes using de novo variants from 12,007 trios and assessed mutation density across 76,215 genome-sequenced individuals in gnomAD, then convened an expert panel to draft classification guidance specific to spliceosomal snRNA genes.

## Relevance to Atlas

Directly relevant: these are noncoding genes implicated in neurodevelopmental disorders, and the paper supplies the background mutation rate that any burden test in snRNA genes has to be nulled against. That base rate is the piece usually missing. Pairs with the ASD prefrontal cortex spliceosome finding logged the same day (PMID 42550007); two independent signals pointing at snRNA biology on one day is a coincidence worth noting, not evidence.

## Genes

None from the 49-gene autism set.

## Methods

De novo variant analysis in 12,007 trios; gnomAD mutation density across 76,215 genomes; expert panel consensus guidance.
