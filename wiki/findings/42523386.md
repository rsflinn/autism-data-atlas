# Sex-specific developmental phenotypes and their response to neonatal Dyrk1a reduction in the Ts65Dn Down syndrome mouse model.

## Key Findings
Restoring Dyrk1a to two copies in a trisomic background rescued some early developmental phenotypes in a sex-specific way but left most abnormalities intact. The authors attribute the partial effect to dysregulation between Dyrk1a RNA and DYRK1A protein levels, contributions from other trisomic genes, or effects limited to adulthood.

## Relevance to Atlas
A dosage-correction experiment that mostly fails is more informative than one that succeeds: it argues that single-gene dosage restoration is insufficient when multiple genes are perturbed together, which is the combinatorial premise this project works from. DYRK1A is in the 49-gene set. Mouse model, and Ts65Dn carries non-Hsa21 material, so generalization is limited.

## Genes
DYRK1A

## Methods
Ts65Dn mice with genetic Dyrk1a copy number normalization, P3-P21 physical/motor/behavioral milestone battery, isolation-induced ultrasonic vocalizations, sex-stratified analysis.
