# 16p11.2 microdeletion enhances gene expression variability between human iPSC-derived forebrain interneuron progenitor cells in culture.

## Key Findings
Single-cell RNA sequencing of isogenic human iPSC-derived ventral telencephalic interneuron progenitors differing only by a heterozygous 16p11.2 microdeletion. Heterozygous cells showed significantly greater transcript-level variability between cells than wild-type, across regulons and random gene sets, with the most variable regulons enriched for cell-cycle genes and controlled by transcription factors associated with autism and obesity. Authors propose the 16p11.2 locus has a genome-wide role in stabilizing transcription.

## Relevance to Atlas
Directly relevant to the atlas question of how a single locus perturbs regulatory networks in a specific cell type (GABAergic interneuron progenitors). The finding is increased transcriptional variability, not a directional shift in named atlas genes; 16p11.2 does not overlap the 49-gene set. Isogenic scRNA-seq design is a reusable methodological template.

## Methods
scRNA-seq of isogenic human iPSC-derived ventral telencephalic interneuron progenitors (2D culture), WT vs heterozygous 16p11.2 microdeletion; comparative bioinformatics of transcript variability across regulons and random gene sets.
