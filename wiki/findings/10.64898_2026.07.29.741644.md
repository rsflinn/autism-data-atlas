# Activation of Neurotensin Receptor 1 in entorhinal cortex rescues olfactory generalization in two mouse models of autism

## Key Findings

Pharmacological activation of neurotensin receptor 1 restored the ability of both Cntnap2-/- and Shank3B+/- mice to recognize learned target odors against novel odor backgrounds, and the entorhinal cortex was identified as the principal site mediating the effect.

## Relevance to Atlas

Two atlas genes with different molecular functions converge on the same sensory-generalization deficit and respond to the same intervention, which is a convergence observation at the circuit level rather than the transcriptional level. Convergence in this project has repeatedly turned out to be dataset-dependent, so the useful test would be whether Ntsr1 activation also rescues models outside these two genotypes.

## Genes

SHANK3, CNTNAP2

## Methods

Mouse behavioral odor generalization assays; pharmacological Ntsr1 activation; region-specific manipulation.
