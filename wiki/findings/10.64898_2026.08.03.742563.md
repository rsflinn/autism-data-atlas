# Autism-associated NRXN1α deletion rewires the H3K27me3 landscape and epigenetically disrupts human neural induction.

## Key Findings

Multi-omic profiling at day 3 of neural induction compared iPSCs from one individual with a biallelic NRXN1 deletion (three clones) against three control lines, combining RNA-seq, ATAC-seq and H3K27me3 ChIP-seq. The deletion was associated with a rewired H3K27me3 landscape at a developmental stage that precedes synaptogenesis by a wide margin.

## Relevance to Atlas

NRXN1 is in the atlas 49-gene set. This is a coding CNV whose reported consequence is repressive-chromatin remodeling, which is the kind of regulatory readout the atlas tracks. Design limit: one deletion carrier versus three unrelated controls, so line-of-origin and genetic background are confounded with genotype and no null distribution across multiple carriers is available. Treat as hypothesis-generating.

## Genes

NRXN1

## Methods

iPSC neural induction to day 3; RNA-seq with DESeq2 adjusted for sex; rMATS splicing; ATAC-seq with TOBIAS footprinting; H3K27me3 ChIP-seq with DiffBind.
