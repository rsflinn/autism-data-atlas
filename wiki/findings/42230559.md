# Dup15q model mice exhibit impaired gastrointestinal motility and a constipation-like phenotype, alleviated by prucalopride.

## Key Findings
Dup15q (a dosage CNV over the GABRB3/GABRA5/GABRG3 cluster) mouse model shows GI dysmotility with elevated colonic GABA-receptor expression; prucalopride rescues transit and social behavior. Touches GABRB3 (49-gene set) via copy-number dosage, not noncoding regulatory variation; mechanistic/therapeutic rather than variant-level.

## Relevance to Atlas
Dosage of the 15q GABA-A cluster (incl. GABRB3) links a structural variant to both behavioral and GI phenotypes -- a dosage data point for a gene-set member; not a noncoding-variant finding.

## Genes
GABRB3

## Methods
Colon qPCR; whole-gut transit (carmine red); ex vivo video motility; bicuculline pharmacology; 6-day prucalopride dosing with social testing.
