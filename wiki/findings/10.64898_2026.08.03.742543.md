# Rescue schizophrenia-related phenotypes caused by Setd1a deficiency by histone demethylase inhibitors

## Key Findings

Conditional Setd1a knockout mice were used to map brain region-specific consequences of SETD1A loss. The dorsal striatum and mediodorsal thalamus showed transcriptomic and neuronal alterations distinct from those previously described in prefrontal cortex, and histone demethylase inhibitors rescued schizophrenia-related phenotypes.

## Relevance to Atlas

Logged for the region-specificity point rather than the disorder. A chromatin regulator produces different downstream programs in different brain regions, which cuts against reading any single-region transcriptomic result as the effect of losing that gene. Schizophrenia model, not autism.

## Methods

Conditional Setd1a knockout mice; region-resolved transcriptomics; histone demethylase inhibitor treatment.
