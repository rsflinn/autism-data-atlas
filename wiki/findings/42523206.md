# A consensus atlas of human brain development defines cell type-specific maturation trajectories across the lifespan.

## Key Findings
Nine human brain development single-cell datasets were reprocessed from raw reads into one taxonomy covering ~2.2 million cells and 156 donors spanning neurogenesis to adulthood. Cell type assignments replicated across heterogeneous source datasets, and the authors derive maturation-predictive gene modules and cortical layer localization over developmental time.

## Relevance to Atlas
A cross-dataset reference with prenatal and postnatal coverage is directly useful for this project's recurring problem that convergence findings are dataset-dependent, and for testing prenatal-versus-postnatal expression reversals of the kind seen for MECP2 in BrainVar. Value here is as infrastructure, not as a finding.

## Genes
None of the 49-gene autism set are directly implicated.

## Methods
Uniform reprocessing of nine published scRNA-seq datasets from raw reads, unified cell-type taxonomy, replicability scoring across datasets, maturation module derivation.
