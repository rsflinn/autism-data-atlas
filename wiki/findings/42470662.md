# Environmental and treatment-related factors associated with developmental outcomes in genetic epilepsies.

## Key Findings
Cross-sectional analysis of 970 individuals across 93 NDDs in the Simons Searchlight dataset (67% with epilepsy). Individuals with epilepsy had lower adaptive function (Vineland-3). Environmental exposures explained more outcome variance than genetic etiology alone (additional R2=19.6%), and the added variance was gene-specific, ranging from ~15% (SCN2A, SLC6A1) to >30% (16p11.2 deletion); ~60% of variance remained unexplained.

## Relevance to Atlas
Speaks to the severity-disruption-disconnect workstream: same genotype, large unexplained outcome variance, with environment explaining more than genetic etiology alone. Uses the public Simons Searchlight dataset and covers SCN2A and SLC6A1 (both in the 49-gene set). No regulatory-variant content.
