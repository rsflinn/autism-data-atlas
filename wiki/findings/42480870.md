# Non-Coding RNA Dysregulation in Autism Spectrum Disorder: Mechanisms, Biomarkers, and Therapeutic Perspectives.

## Key Findings
Narrative review cataloguing non-coding RNAs (miRNAs, lncRNAs, circRNAs, piRNAs) reported as dysregulated in ASD cell lines, brain tissue and blood. Describes competing endogenous RNA networks in which miRNAs and lncRNAs regulate ASD genes including SHANK3, SYNGAP1 and CHD8, and affect NMDA receptor signaling. No new data; authors note biomarker utility depends on replication in larger multi-center cohorts.

## Relevance to Atlas
Adds a regulatory (RNA-level) layer connecting three atlas genes (SHANK3, SYNGAP1, CHD8) to ncRNA control and NMDA receptor signaling. Descriptive/review only; no test against a null, so it supports hypothesis generation rather than confirmation.

## Genes
SHANK3, SYNGAP1, CHD8

## Methods
Narrative review of published ncRNA expression studies; no new data.
