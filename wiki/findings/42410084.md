# Nonsense-mediated decay influences position-dependent effects of SCN2A premature stop codons on neuronal excitability and behavior.

## Key Findings
Two mouse lines carrying patient-derived SCN2A premature stop codons show that nonsense-mediated decay makes truncating variants act differently depending on position, challenging the assumption that all SCN2A truncations cause uniform haploinsufficiency. Bears on how one ASD-risk ion channel gene produces variable effects on neuronal excitability.
