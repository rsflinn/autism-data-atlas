# The independent and joint effects of outdoor air pollution exposure and genetic risk on mental health trajectories during adolescence.

## Key Findings
PM2.5 exposure and polygenic risk for depression jointly shaped psychotic-like experience trajectories in adolescent females, with sex-specific genetic effects on internalising symptoms.

## Relevance to Atlas
Low autism specificity. No autism outcome is measured. Cleared the numeric threshold on cohort size. Logged as a template for polygenic-by-environment interaction testing in a large longitudinal cohort, which this project has not attempted.

## Genes
None of the 49-gene autism set are directly implicated.

## Methods
ABCD Study, PM2.5 exposure estimates, PRS-SCZ and PRS-MDD, sex-stratified longitudinal mixed models, FDR correction.
