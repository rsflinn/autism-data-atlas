# Age-stratified individual-specific subspace of autism spectrum disorder based on common orthogonal basis extraction algorithm improves the accuracy of clinical symptoms prediction.

## Key Findings
Using 437 participants (208 ASD, 229 typically developing) from ABIDE, the common orthogonal basis extraction algorithm isolated age-stratified individual-specific functional connectivity features. These features improved prediction of behavioral symptoms, with the largest gain for adolescent social behavior, and replicated in an independent sample.

## Relevance to Atlas
Included for its use of the public ABIDE cohort and phenotype prediction. It carries no regulatory or molecular content, so its relevance to the atlas is peripheral, tied to the imaging dataset and to age-stratified phenotype modeling rather than to mechanism.
