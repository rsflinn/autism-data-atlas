# Diversity of molecular karyotypes of 234 pediatric patients with autism spectrum disorder in chromosomal microarray analysis.

## Key Findings

CMA on 234 pediatric ASD cases; pathogenic/likely-pathogenic CNV yield 8.55%, replicating known ASD CNV loci (15q13.3, 15q11.2, 22q11.21) in an independent cohort. KMT2A (49-gene set) sits in a recurrent 11q23.3 duplication. Structural/dosage data relevant to the atlas; standard clinical CMA, no cell-type resolution.
