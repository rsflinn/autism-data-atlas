# Individualized antisense oligonucleotides for SCN2A-related developmental epileptic encephalopathy.

## Key Findings
Two n=1 clinical studies of allele-selective antisense oligonucleotides targeting heterozygous intronic SNPs to lower mutant SCN2A transcript while preserving the wild-type copy in two boys with SCN2A-related developmental and epileptic encephalopathy. Seizure frequency fell 26% and 90%, concomitant medication use decreased, and neurodevelopmental skills improved; both ASOs were well tolerated. Haplotype phasing found 16% of a separate SCN2A-RD infant cohort carry compatible SNPs.

## Relevance to Atlas
Provides an SCN2A (atlas gene) example where the actionable variant is intronic/noncoding and allele-selective knockdown is tuned to a heterozygous SNP. Clinical n=1 evidence; relevant to the autism-epilepsy comorbidity boundary and to noncoding-variant interpretation, not to population architecture.

## Genes
SCN2A

## Methods
Two parallel n=1 clinical studies; allele-selective ASOs targeting heterozygous intronic SNPs; endpoints seizure frequency and neurodevelopment; haplotype phasing in a separate SCN2A-RD infant cohort (rapid WGS). ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06314490.
