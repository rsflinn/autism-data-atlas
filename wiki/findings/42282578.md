# Social Novelty Recruits a Dysfunctional Nucleus Accumbens Ensemble That Drives Social Avoidance in a Shank3 Autism Model.

## Key Findings

In a Shank3 mouse model, nucleus accumbens social ensembles encode social avoidance rather than approach, and silencing them during social novelty restores social investigation. Cell-ensemble-resolved circuit mechanism for SHANK3 (49-gene set). Descriptive mechanism, no variant data; abstract had gene-name redaction artifacts.
