# Brain structure abnormalities in neurodevelopmental synaptopathies.

## Key Findings
MRI volumetry in patients with SYNGAP1 (n=19), GRIN-family (n=19) and STXBP1 (n=10) variants, three disorders affecting glutamatergic transmission. Shared changes included larger basal ganglia, thalamus and ventricles and reduced white matter, cerebellar and limbic volumes; ventricular enlargement correlated with severity of intellectual disability, language and motor impairment. Amygdala volume deficits were most prominent in SYNGAP1 and GRINpathies.

## Relevance to Atlas
Feeds the severity-disruption workstream: three glutamatergic-synaptopathy genes (SYNGAP1, GRIN2A/GRIN2B, STXBP1) with quantitative severity correlations. Imaging phenotype, not regulatory variation; useful as phenotype/severity reference, not as evidence about noncoding architecture.

## Genes
SYNGAP1, GRIN2A, GRIN2B, STXBP1

## Methods
MRI volumetry with automated segmentation; SYNGAP1 (n=19), GRIN family (n=19), STXBP1 (n=10) vs age-matched normative templates; parametric/non-parametric tests, ANCOVA; correlation with clinical severity.
