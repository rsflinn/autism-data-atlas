# Prenatal cell-free DNA methylome detects association with autism and maternal obesity.

## Key Findings
Whole-genome bisulfite sequencing of cell-free DNA from third-trimester maternal plasma finds differentially methylated regions distinguishing ASD-associated pregnancies, enriched for synaptic functions and known ASD genes and recapitulating DMRs seen in placenta and postmortem cortex. Noncoding epigenetic signal detectable prenatally.
