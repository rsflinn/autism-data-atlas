# Rare Variants in Purinergic P2X Receptor Genes in Individuals With Autism Spectrum Disorder: An Exploratory Study.

## Key Findings
Retrospective WES review of individuals diagnosed with ASD. Eleven individuals carried heterozygous variants in purinergic P2X receptor genes (P2RX4, P2RX5, P2RX7), including an in-frame deletion (p.Ile322del), a truncating variant (p.Tyr366LeufsTer7) and a canonical splice-site variant (c.614+1G>A). Core clinical features across all genes were ASD, intellectual disability and epilepsy. Authors state findings are hypothesis-generating and lack segregation and functional validation.

## Relevance to Atlas
Adds candidate ion-channel genes (ATP-gated P2X receptors) to the ASD+epilepsy overlap picture. None are in the 49-gene set, and variants are coding not noncoding, but the ASD/ID/epilepsy co-occurrence is on-theme for the ion-channel final-common-pathway line. Exploratory, no functional validation.
