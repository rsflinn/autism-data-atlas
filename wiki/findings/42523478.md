# Convergent Inhibitory Cortical Circuit Disruption Drives Genetically Distinct Absence Seizures.

## Key Findings
Two genetically distinct absence epilepsy mutants converge on disruption of the same layer 6 corticothalamic to Tac1 interneuron circuit motif. Chemogenetically disrupting that motif in wild-type mice was sufficient to reproduce the downstream somatostatin interneuron collapse and generate spike-wave discharges, establishing serial causality rather than correlation.

## Relevance to Atlas
This is the strongest form of the convergence argument: different causal genes, one shared circuit, plus a sufficiency test in wild-type animals. It is a useful methodological template for how this project would need to test convergence claims, which so far have rested on correlational overlap. Tottering carries a Cacna1a mutation, in the 49-gene set.
