# Roles of histone lysine methylation in neurodevelopment and related disorders.

## Key Findings
Review of how H3K4 histone methylation marks shape chromatin accessibility and transcription during neural development, and how their disruption drives neurodevelopmental disorders. Background for the chromatin-regulatory atlas genes (KMT2A, KDM6B).
