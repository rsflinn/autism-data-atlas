# CDKL5 deficiency results in atypical subregion-specific expression of perineuronal nets during mouse visual critical period.

## Key Findings
CDKL5-null mice show precocious, subregion-specific perineuronal net changes across primary visual cortex during the critical period, potentially relevant to the cortical visual impairment seen in CDKL5 deficiency disorder.

## Relevance to Atlas
Cell-type-resolved (PV+ GABAergic) developmental phenotype for an atlas core gene (CDKL5). Mechanistic/descriptive, no regulatory-variant content.

## Genes
CDKL5

## Methods
CDKL5-null male mice; WFA staining of perineuronal nets across V1 subregions at P15 and P30.
