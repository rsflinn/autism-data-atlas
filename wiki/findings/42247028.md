# Neurobehavioral Impacts of the Autism Risk Gene, WAC: Studies Involving C. elegans and Mice.

## Key Findings
WAC deletion in C. elegans and mice alters cholinergic, dopaminergic and motility behaviors; enhanced nicotinic acetylcholine receptor activity in worms and an inverse Wac mRNA-CHRNA7 relationship in mouse cortex.

## Relevance to Atlas
WAC is an established ASD/DeSanto-Shinawi gene already tracked in the atlas (prior WAC vertebrate-model entry). Adds a cholinergic-circuit mechanism but no noncoding/regulatory-variant data.

## Genes
None from 49-gene set

## Methods
C. elegans wac mutants (aldicarb assay, RNAi), Wac mouse cortical expression analysis.
