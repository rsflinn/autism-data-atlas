# Region-specific microglial alterations in the acoustic startle circuit in Shank3 mice.

## Key Findings
Immunofluorescence of Shank3 knockout vs wild-type mice across six auditory/tactile regions found a 39% IBA1 increase in central amygdala (p=0.01) and 13% higher microglial density in caudal pontine reticular nucleus (p=0.02), without robust morphological activation or CD68 change, suggesting circuit-dependent microglial alterations.

## Relevance to Atlas
