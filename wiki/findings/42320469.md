# Single-cell profiling of DNA methylation in autism spectrum disorder prefrontal cortex reveals distinct regulatory and aging signatures.

## Key Findings
snmCT-seq profiled DNA methylation and transcript levels in more than 60,000 prefrontal cortex nuclei from 49 donors. The study identified over 30,000 ASD differentially methylated regions enriched in promoters and cell-type-specific regulatory elements active across the lifespan. ASD methylation changes were uncorrelated with transcript levels and small in magnitude relative to age effects, which concentrated in excitatory neurons.

## Relevance to Atlas
Directly relevant to the atlas's regulatory focus: it maps ASD-associated epigenetic regulation to specific cortical cell types and to promoters and regulatory elements, and quantifies it against an age baseline. The null framing matters here: the authors report ASD methylation effects are uncorrelated with expression and small compared with aging, so this is a regulatory-landscape resource rather than a causal-variant finding.

## Genes
None of the 49-gene autism set are named in the abstract.

## Methods
Single-nucleus methylation plus transcriptome (snmCT-seq); differentially methylated region calling; CG versus non-CG methylation analysis; lifespan/age modeling.
