# A Novel Variant of the CHD8 Gene in a Patient with Autism Spectrum Disorder.

## Key Findings
Clinical exome sequencing in a female with ASD and macrocephaly identifies a novel maternally inherited CHD8 variant; blood RNA analysis probes its functional and splicing effect. Adds a CHD8 case to the atlas gene set.

## Relevance to Atlas
