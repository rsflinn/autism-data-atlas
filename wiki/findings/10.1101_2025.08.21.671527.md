# Learning-Associated Flexibility of Cortical Taste Coding Is Impaired in Shank3 Knockout Mice

## Key Findings

Two-photon calcium imaging during conditioned taste aversion learning tested whether Shank3 deletion changes how gustatory cortex updates taste representations. The question is whether the sensory-processing differences seen in autism models extend to the flexibility of sensory encoding, not just its baseline properties.

## Relevance to Atlas

SHANK3 is in the atlas 49-gene set. The framing is about flexibility of encoding rather than a static deficit, which is a different phenotype axis than most gene-level work in the atlas. Single-gene mouse model, no human data, no regulatory content.

## Genes

SHANK3

## Methods

Two-photon calcium imaging in gustatory cortex during conditioned taste aversion in Shank3 knockout mice.
