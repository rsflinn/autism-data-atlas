# Aberrant expression of stress-related Hsrω-n lncRNA contributes to CGG repeat-mediated toxicity in a Drosophila model of FXTAS

## Key Findings

In a Drosophila model of fragile X-associated tremor/ataxia syndrome, expanded CGG repeats drove overexpression of the stress-related long noncoding RNA Hsr-omega-nuclear. Raising its expression enhanced CGG-induced neurotoxicity and reducing it suppressed toxicity.

## Relevance to Atlas

A noncoding RNA acting as a dose-sensitive modifier of a repeat expansion phenotype. The mechanism sits in the same conceptual space as the atlas working model, where noncoding elements tune the severity of a primary lesion rather than causing disease alone. Drosophila, adult-onset neurodegeneration, no direct autism relevance.
