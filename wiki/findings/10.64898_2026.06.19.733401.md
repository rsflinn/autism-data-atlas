# Brain structural and genetic correlates of motor coordination and learning behaviours: modelling developmental coordination disorder

## Key Findings

Using the BXD recombinant inbred mouse panel across 14 strains, mice with a developmental coordination disorder-like behavioral profile showed modestly reduced total brain volume with enlarged cortical regions alongside reduced cerebellar, thalamic and other subcortical volumes.

## Relevance to Atlas

A polygenic rather than single-gene modeling approach to a neurodevelopmental phenotype, which is closer to the atlas working model than knockout studies. Fourteen strains is a small panel for genetic mapping, so structural correlations should be read as descriptive.

## Genes

None from the 49-gene autism set.

## Methods

BXD recombinant inbred panel; high-resolution ex vivo MRI; behavioral motor coordination and learning assays.
