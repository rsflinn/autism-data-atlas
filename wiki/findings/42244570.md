# The Regional Vulnerability Index (RVI) as a Neuroimaging-Based Biomarker for Autism: Associations with Likelihood, Cognition, and Longitudinal Social Outcomes.

## Key Findings
RVI-ASD, a brain-pattern similarity metric built from ENIGMA-ASD and COCORO, shows a larger case-control effect (d=0.30) than any single measure, is stable and heritable (h2=0.55-0.83) in ABCD, and tracks cognition and longitudinal social outcomes.

## Relevance to Atlas
Large-cohort heritability and phenotype-tracking biomarker relevant to the severity/phenotype workstream and the common-variant liability framing. Uses public ABCD/ENIGMA data. No gene- or variant-level content.

## Genes
None from 49-gene set

## Methods
Composite vulnerability index from ENIGMA-ASD effect sizes + COCORO DTI; validation in ENIGMA-ASD (N=2,577) and ABCD (N=4,201) with heritability and longitudinal modeling.
