# Non-coding Regulatory Variants in ASD (Autism Spectrum Disorders) Disrupt CTCF Domains and Shape Cell-Type-Specific Neurodevelopmental Landscapes Revealed by Single-Cell Analyses and Cortical Organoids.

## Key Findings

Targeted sequencing of 85,394 regulatory elements in 200 coding-negative ASD trios. De novo regulatory variants concentrate in promoters and CTCF boundaries and map to dynamically expressed genes (POGZ, NFIB, ROCK2) that overlap known ASD coding-risk genes; inherited variants hit CTCF-binding sites and constitutively expressed genes. Directly on-target for the atlas: a coding-negative cohort, regulatory-variant focus, and cell-type/stage-resolved expression. Association-level, preprint, not independently validated.
