# Maternal anti-collapsin response mediator protein 1 antibody inducing autism-like behaviors in offspring.

## Key Findings
Maternal anti-CRMP1 antibody produces ASD-like behavior in mouse offspring and reduces expression of presynaptic-organization genes including Nrxn1 and Pten (both in the 49-gene set). Mechanism is autoantibody-driven (environmental), not variant-level; relevance is convergence on the same synaptic genes via a non-genetic route.

## Relevance to Atlas
Shows two 49-set genes (NRXN1, PTEN) downregulated downstream of an environmental insult -- a convergence-on-synaptic-genes data point, not evidence about regulatory variants.

## Genes
NRXN1, PTEN

## Methods
Cell-based antibody assay; behavioral battery; P30 cortex bulk transcriptomics with qPCR validation; iPSC-derived neuron growth cone assay.
