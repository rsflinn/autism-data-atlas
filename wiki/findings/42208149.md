# An Adnp frameshift variant disrupts Wnt signalling inducing chromatocytoskeletal defects and autism-related behaviour in male mice.

## Key Findings
Heterozygous de novo variants in ADNP cause Helsmoortel-Van der Aa syndrome (HVDAS). A CRISPR 14-bp deletion (p.Leu822Hisfs*6) Adnp mouse model reduced Adnp levels and chromatin association, with a genome-wide increase in chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq), downregulation of Wnt signalling (RNA-seq), locus-specific 3D genome changes (Hi-C), disrupted Camk2a/Dbn1 interactions, synaptic deficits, and autism-related behaviour.

## Relevance to Atlas
CRISPR Adnp frameshift mouse model (HVDAS) shows genome-wide increased chromatin accessibility, Wnt pathway downregulation, and locus-specific 3D genome changes alongside autism-related behaviour. ADNP is in the 49-gene set.
