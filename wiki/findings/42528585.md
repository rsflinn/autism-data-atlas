# Shared genetic and molecular architecture between insulin resistance and cognitive performance.

## Key Findings
Insulin-resistance traits and cognitive measures share low-to-moderate, mostly opposite-direction genetic correlation, with 696 fine-mapped shared candidate causal variants and mixed local effect directions.

## Relevance to Atlas
Low autism specificity. No autism phenotype is analyzed. Cleared the numeric threshold on dataset size and fine-mapping method. Method-relevant only, as an example of multivariate fine-mapping across correlated traits with directionally mixed local genetic correlations.

## Genes
None of the 49-gene autism set are directly implicated.

## Methods
LDSC-style global and local genetic correlation, multivariate Sum of Single Effects fine-mapping, positional and eQTL mapping, drug-gene interaction annotation.
